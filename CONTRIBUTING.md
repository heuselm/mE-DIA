# Contributing to mE-DIA

The rules are in the README: [Contributing](README.md#contributing). In short:

1. **Issue first.** Use the *Feature request* or *Bug report* form. Feature requests give the
   outline, purpose and proposed approach. Bug reports give a description, reproduction
   steps and the execution environment.
2. **Agree on the approach** in the issue before larger changes.
3. **Pull request** referencing the issue (`Closes #N`), with the intended change or fix, the
   tests performed (`bin/test_*.py` output, plus data and before/after numbers for pipeline
   changes), and whether existing results change.

Contributions are accepted under the project's license ([LICENSE](LICENSE), PolyForm
Noncommercial 1.0.0).
